## Key numerical sources

- Auger spectrum parameters: Pierre Auger Collab., PRL 125 (2020) 121106 and PRD 102 (2020) 062005 (`Bibliografia/augerSpectrum2020.pdf`).
- Muon deficit: Pierre Auger Collab., PRL 117 (2016) 192001.
- Dipole significance 6.8σ and ~50 000 events above 8 EeV in 19 yr, exposure 123 000 km² sr yr: Pierre Auger Collab., ICRC 2025 proceedings (arXiv:2507.19243) and ICRC 2024 contribution.
- Auger collaboration size (~400 scientists, 17 countries): auger.org.
- QUBIC detectors: QUBIC IV, arXiv:2101.06787 (256 NbSi TES, T_c ≈ 410 mK, bath 320 mK, 128 SQUIDs at 1 K, SiGe ASIC at 40 K, NEP). QUBIC I, arXiv:2011.02213 (150/220 GHz, σ(r) = 0.015 target, site opacity).
- QUBIC status and ITeDA role: CONICET news (24/11/2022); argentina.gob.ar (CNEA/ITeDA, "Observatorio QUBIC" and the 2025 mount completion note); F. Sánchez 2026 slides.
- HERON: arXiv:2507.04382; <https://www.iteda.gob.ar/proyecto-heron/>; <https://www.argentina.gob.ar/cnea/ip/instituto-de-tecnologias-en-deteccion-y-astroparticulas-iteda/proyecto-heron>.
- Nobel Prize in Physics 2026: NobelPrize.org press release (6 Oct 2026); IceCube/UW–Madison news.
- GW170817 timeline: LIGO–Virgo et al., ApJL 848 (2017) L12. Neutrino follow-up: ANTARES, IceCube, Auger, ApJL 850 (2017) L35.
- KM3-230213A: KM3NeT Collab., Nature 638 (2025) 376.
- BICEP/Keck: PRL 127 (2021) 151301 (r < 0.036).
- Planck 2018 parameters: A&A 641 (2020) A6.
