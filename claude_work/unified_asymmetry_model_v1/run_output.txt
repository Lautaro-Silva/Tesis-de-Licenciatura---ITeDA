The '+B&B muon attenuation' columns are KEPT BELOW FOR RECORD ONLY, NOT AS A FINDING.
Two problems, found by the author's direct questioning of the physics, retract this candidate:
  1. Population/threshold mismatch: B&B's Fig.1 is raw AIRES particle output -- essentially
     the ENTIRE muon spectrum, the SD's own near-zero-threshold population -- not the UMD's

     population the UMD never sees into the UMD's own prediction.
  2. Likely double-counting: B&B's own text ('total decreases, but spread increases')
     describes lateral spread of the muon population with depth -- i.e. very plausibly the
     SAME physics as the kinematic-divergence term already in the 'no attenuation' combination
     above (properly spectrum-weighted, prior work). Adding B&B's cruder, energy-integrated
     number on top double-counts that physics with the opposite sign from the better source.
